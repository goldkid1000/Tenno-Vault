💎 How to price-check

1. Use the live price checker on this site, or open warframe.market
2. Look at the cheapest in-game sellers — that's the real market price
3. Vaulted (unavailable) items cost more — check if the relic is vaulted
4. Full prime sets sell for more than individual parts
5. Never trust trade-chat prices alone — always cross-check

🤝 How to sell, step by step

1. Farm relics (best relic farms) and crack them in fissures
2. Price-check each rare part with the price checker
3. Post WTS in trade chat, or list on warframe.market for buyers to find you
4. When someone whispers, invite them to your dojo
5. Meet at the trading post, double-check the items in the window, confirm
6. Daily trade limit = your Mastery Rank (MR10 = 10 trades/day)

⚠️ Don't get scammed

• The trade window shows exactly what you're giving and getting — read it before confirming.
• Nobody from "DE support" will ever ask for your password — that's always a scam.
• If someone swaps items at the last second, the window highlights the change — cancel.
• Riven prices are subjective — check multiple listings before buying.
• Never trade outside the game's trade window (no "send plat first" deals).
